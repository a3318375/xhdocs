---
title: "pgvector 向量库"
description: "Postgres + pgvector 容器，给 RAG 用"
---

比较低配的选择，主要是考虑和业务共用

```yaml
services:
  postgres:
    image: pgvector/pgvector:pg18-trixie
    container_name: postgres
    restart: always
    network_mode: bridge
    ports:
      - 5433:5432
    environment:
      POSTGRES_USER: postgres
      POSTGRES_PASSWORD: <已打码>
networks: {}

```

标签有很多种，可以独立指定向量库和pg库的版本，感兴趣自己看
